Published observations

Keep the units and timing.

The competitive aim is more useful delivery per radio second. The figures below are different experiments, so each carries its own conditions.

30 October source review: pipeline ideas, without a speed ranking.

The review of MeshCore companion v1.18.0, Reticulum 1.5.5 and RNode firmware 1.86 found no matched startup-inclusive LoRa file benchmark. RNode supports SF5 on compatible radios; its displayed on-air bitrate and the later-starting rncp transfer timer differ from useful file goodput including startup. MCU-owned scheduling, missing-part requests and overlapping preparation inform our next pipeline work. Reaching our 3 kB/s SF5 goal would not alone prove either upstream slower. Read the pinned primary sources and comparison requirements.

24 October: a measured improvement within Meshticulum.

At about 20 metres B → A, fresh 8,164-byte verified files reached 931.254611 B/s synchronously and 1,084.761833 B/s with the MCU DATA queue. Both intervals run from sender startup through authenticated receipt: 8.766668 and 7.526076 seconds. The 16.483915% throughput gain comes from one fixed-order pair at SF7/BW500/CR4:5, 921.375 MHz and 0 dBm, with zero retries, receive drops or new unknown outcomes. Checked drained endpoint completion followed queued receipt by 27.456 ms. The earlier about-20-metre LoRa sample remains 888.965 B/s; the queued result is 22.025263% higher, with different firmware. These observations do not change the upstream comparison's workloads, timing boundaries or security scope. Read the pair and queue limits · Inspect the result.

The public SDK GFSK results have separate conditions.

On 17 October 2026, two fresh 100,000-byte files reached 1.95–2.03 kB/s from first sender startup through final authenticated receipt, or 1.76–1.86 kB/s through endpoint closure. On 18 October, final fresh 250 kB, 500 kB and 1 MB files reached 2.111, 2.216 and 2.230 kB/s including startup, with all parts and whole files independently verified. All used direct GFSK250 about one metre apart. These are separate from the LoRa table and do not establish a general ranking. Read the SDK timing boundaries and remaining work.

Matched LoRa profile result.

Six same-payload pairs at BW500/CR4/5 measured median sender-ARM-to-receipt rates of 1.103 kB/s (8.820 kbit/s) at SF7 and 0.720 kB/s (5.759 kbit/s) at SF8. SF8 took 1.531× as long on this near-bench link. All 12 chunks and receipts verified. The MCU's 1,024-attempt cap stood at 380 after both campaigns, leaving 644 attempts of headroom, with zero receive drops. This is a profile comparison, not a field-range or upstream ranking.

A scoped 2.61× numerical ratio.

1,071.3 / 410 is approximately 2.61. Windowed resource flow and individually acknowledged frames have different payloads and timing boundaries. Two consecutive Meshticulum finite samples exceeded 1 kB/s (8 kbit/s); they do not establish sustained capacity, equal range or stronger encryption security.
