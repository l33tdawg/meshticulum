01 / Why Meshticulum

The radio is small.
The ambition shouldn’t be.

A useful network needs both practical radio operation and secure ways to move complete data. Meshticulum brings those priorities together in a separate protocol.

A starting point

MeshCore

A lightweight packet-radio framework, with embedded companion and repeater roles. Its practical radio focus is part of what inspires this work.

MeshCore project ↗
A starting point

Reticulum

A cryptographic networking stack spanning local and wide-area networks, including very low-bandwidth links. Its broader networking model informs the other side of the design.

Reticulum overview ↗
The bridge we’re building

Meshticulum

Bounded radio transfers, authenticated peer and service bindings, verified resources, and an explicit path policy that can use LoRa or IP.

Compare the designs ↗

This is a bridge between design strengths. MeshCore and Reticulum wire compatibility is not implemented.

02 / Architecture

Trust above.
Transport below.

A resource keeps its identity when its path changes. Each transfer still binds the peers, service, limits and original deadline.

01

Move useful bytes

Compact resource blocks, receiver credit and selective acknowledgements reduce avoidable framing and per-frame overhead.

02

Verify before accepting

Public resources use signed manifests and content hashes. Private envelopes retain recipient encryption and sender signatures.

03

Keep the original limits

Deadlines, replay decisions and attempt debt survive cancellation and restart. A retry does not renew permission.

A comparison you can inspect

The earlier LoRa sample at 1,071.3 B/s is numerically 2.61× the 410 B/s contributed test in MeshCore issue #3404. That test used 25 individually acknowledged 128-byte frames at SF7/BW500. Ours used a windowed resource reply. Different workloads and timing boundaries prevent a general speed ranking.

Installed and continued.

The 17 October alpha's clean installed-wheel check completed another 100 kB file at 1.74 kB/s (13.9 kbit/s), including sender startup and a five-second pause between verified parts. All ten endpoint processes closed successfully. Read the historical installation and continuation check. The original alpha prerelease retains that proof; alpha.2 has separate artifact and clean-install checks. Field range, wider recovery and native private qualification remain open.
